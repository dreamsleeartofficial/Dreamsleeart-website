03 / THE ARTIST

DREAMSLEEART

Dreamsleeart is a 20-year-old storyteller and visual artist focused on creating imaginative worlds through manga, illustration, comics, and concept art. Drawing inspiration from dreams, fantasy, liminal spaces, and high-energy action, the work explores themes of ambition, wonder, identity, and the unknown. Through dynamic compositions and expressive characters. Aiming to craft stories and concepts that leave a lasting impression long after the page is turned.
